Everything returned to pristine, immaculate perfection. 

Except the Creator. God remained dead, an extinct footnote in the annals of a king who had outgrown the need for heaven.

Godric turned back to me, the cruel tyrant vanishing as though he had never been there, his face glowing once more with that bright, handsome, fatherly warmth that made my soul weep in silent terror. "Well, Osvald, my boy," Godric said gently, reaching down to pull me up from the floor, his massive hands warm, steady, and clean. "That clears up the lineage, doesn't it?
