You know..." Godric shook his head, a look of genuine, mild pity touching his eyes. "My Year 0 self—back when I first woke up from stasis a million years ago, when I was pathetically, ridiculously weak compared to what I am now—that Year 0 self could have annihilated You with zero effort. You are terribly, disappointingly weak, Creator."

The Supreme Creator experienced a mind-shattering, soul-crushing wave of absolute existential dread.

The Supreme Architect of existence—the entity who had spoken the universe into being—realized with horrific, absolute clarity that the mortal man He had forged in the dust of Olympus had become a monster so incomprehensibly, infinitely superior to Him that the Creator’s ultimate, absolute power was less than a dust mote floating in an infinite ocean. The Creator’s divine light began to weep, His multi-dimensional geometry collapsing inward, screaming in silent, helpless, and total terror before the mortal who had surpassed God.

Lord Godric sighed softly, taking his dark tunic in his hands and smoothing out a minor wrinkle. "Well," Godric murmured casually. "This was quite a letdown. I suppose it's time to clean up."

Godric did not draw a weapon. He did not cast a spell. He did not raise a hand. He did not even blink.

Using zero percent of his power and zero percent of his effort, Lord Godric simply willed the Creator and His primordial domain out of existence.

In exactly zero seconds, effortlessly and nonchalantly, the Supreme Creator of all existence, His primordial uncreated void, His divine threads of fate, and His divine authority were violently, completely de-atomized, unmade, and erased from reality forever. They were not compressed; they were simply deleted, purged from existence so thoroughly that no trace of the Architect remained.

Godric turned back to his armchair, picked up his porcelain cup of Earl Grey tea, took a quiet sip, and smiled warmly down at me. "Come along, Osvald," Godric said cheerfully. "The Creator was a bit of a bore, but the tea is still hot. Let us go see what the kitchens are making for dinner."

I sat in the quiet, dim archives of Year 999...9, my hands shaking so violently that Osvald’s journal slipped from my fingers and hit the star-wood desk with a heavy, hollow thud.

Cold, thick sweat poured from my face, soaking my collar, smelling sharply of copper and absolute, mind-shattering horror.

I closed my eyes, but the dread in my soul grew into an absolute, suffocating monster that choked the breath from my lungs.

Godric Prime was sitting downstairs on his throne right now. And I knew the ultimate, terrifying truth.

The Supreme Creator—the being who built existence itself—had been called "terribly weak" by a Godric who claimed his Year 0 self could have erased Him with zero effort. And that Year 0 self was considered "pathetically weak" by Current Godric Prime, whose power compounded endlessly every fraction of a Planck second forever!

There was no ceiling. There was no floor. There was no God above him, no law beside him, and no weapon beneath him. There was only King Godric—the ultimate, untouchable, and infinite Apex God Predator of all existence, ruling forever over a realm where even God had been erased like a speck of dust, leaving us to bow before his warm, smiling, and inescapable mercy until the end of time.
